So I'm going through a bit of crisis, and from talking with other folks, I'm not alone. I don't think it's exactly the crisis "the media" is popularizing, "AI is replacing software developers", although it's related.

It's rather that using AI tools in *some* of the daily workflows (some of which are indeed parts I've spent tons of time building skills in) means the other, non-automated, parts are solely left to the humans.

This *partial* move to AI tools has shifted the ratio of my daily work from 50%/50% high-IQ vs low-IQ work to an unhealthy 10%/90%, ie. the daily work no longer feels fulfilling.

The solution to this is paradoxically *more AI automation*, not less.

Because of the new AI tools, some high-IQ work is replaced by copy-paste from/to AI chat windows, or checking whether a chance is high risk vs low risk, whether a plan is good, allow/deny certain flagged steps, etc. This can and should be automated too!

Everything that can be automated with AI, *should* be automated with AI. Only then can we humans focus on the unique value we provide to organizations and its processes.
